# Questions, information, and answers

Three kinds of item are mixed here on purpose, so nothing hides behind a category. **Answered** means an
official document settled it. **Open** means the answer changes the plan and no official document settles it
yet. **Decision** is a choice only you can make.

## Answered from official documents

**Is the house fee on top of the buy-in, or inside it?**
Inside it. Every official structure sheet read here prints the allocation of the buy-in. The Venetian's wording
is explicit: "$58 of the $300 Buy-In is withheld for house fees (Rake + Staff). The prize pool consists of
$242 of every $300 Buy In." The same pattern holds at Wynn ($600 = $510 prize pool + $65 tournament fee + $25
staff charge) and at Bellagio ($600 = $510 prize pool + $50 house fee + $40 optional gratuity). **What you pay
to enter is the buy-in.** The site records the fee as a component and never adds it on top.

**How many single-entry tournaments are there really?**
80, once all 37 November structure sheets and all 15 NYE structure sheets are read. 22 of those are plain
freezeouts with no bounty, satellite or team element. The rest are single entry but carry one of those prize
structures.

**Does a bounty or a satellite count as "multiple entries"?**
No. A bounty or satellite takes one entry like any other freezeout. Only a re-entry, rebuy, add-on,
subsequent-buy-in or multi-flight clause changes the number of entries a player may take. That is the sole
criterion for the single-entry section.

**What does the 8 AM window hold?**
Nothing verified. The earliest confirmed single-entry start in the entire plan is 9:10 AM on Dec 12, and that
is a $260 turbo milestone satellite. The earliest confirmed plain freezeout starts at 10:10 AM. No official
document read here schedules an 8 AM tournament.

**Are the daily tournaments still running during the series?**
Partly. The Wynn daily structure sheets state each event "may be suspended during Seniors Events, Classic
Events, or other Tournament Series." So the Wynn dailies cannot be relied on during the WPT World Championship
or the Signature Series. The Daily feed page carries the published formats and the suspension language.

**How long is a tournament?**
Unknown, and unknowable from the documents. No official source publishes a projected finish time. The printed
late-registration cutoff is the last moment an entry can be taken, and the site uses that as the practical end
of the registration commitment, labelled as derived. Plan on a full evening for a noon start.

**Can the walk from a hotel to a poker room be confirmed?**
Yes, as a computed figure. No Strip resort publishes a walking time. All 42 hotel-to-venue pairs are computed
with one OpenStreetMap pedestrian routing request over the official street addresses, and every pair carries a
Google Maps and an Apple Maps walking-directions link so you can confirm it before booking.

## Open: the document that would settle it does not exist yet

**1. No date-specific room rate for any of the seven hotels.**
Every official booking engine is a JavaScript application that returns only a loading shell to a plain fetch.
No property publishes a rate card. Every stay total in the plan is therefore blank.
*What closes it:* one dated quote per property for the same dates, occupancy and room type, appended to the
Hotel rate log. Two quotes captured the same way become a like-for-like comparison.

**2. Caesars Palace's resort fee rests on a secondary source.**
Caesars' own hotel page carries no fee table. The $54.95 plus tax figure comes from a travel-industry
reseller's description, and is labelled secondary everywhere it appears.
*What closes it:* Caesars' resort-fee page, or a booking confirmation that itemises the fee.

**3. 93 tournament rows still have no confirmed entry rule.**
62 Wynn WPT rows and all 31 Wynn Signature Series rows. The WPT structure PDF extraction stops after the
Dec 3 - 5 event, and the Signature Series structures document contains only the words "LAS VEGAS POKER -
COMING SOON" and no structure at all.
*What closes it:* a complete WPT structures PDF, and the Signature Series structures once Wynn publishes them.

**4. The Bellagio Saturday buy-in is contradictory.**
The official sheet's rule text says $600 with a $510/$50/$40 split; its own heading says "NLH $1100 BUY-IN";
its file name says $1000 NLH; and it refers to a Day 2 in September 2026. The three buy-in figures cannot all
be right.
*What closes it:* a corrected Bellagio Saturday sheet, or the poker room's own answer.

**5. Venetian and Palazzo maximum occupancy is not stated as a number.**
The official terms charge $50 per additional guest aged 13 or over but never state a maximum.
*What closes it:* the room-type page or the booking engine's occupancy selector.

**6. Two Wynn documents extract with shifted columns.**
The WPT schedule and the Signature Series schedule both produce rows where a printed date does not line up
with the printed event name. Those fields were not ingested. Series dates come from the Wynn poker hub instead.
*What closes it:* a rendered-PDF read of the affected pages.

## Decisions for you

**A. Which venue do you build the trip around?**
If the answer is the Venetian DeepStack series, staying at the Venetian or the Palazzo removes the walk
entirely and the poker-player rate starts at $119. If the answer is the WPT World Championship, staying at
Encore removes the walk, and the Venetian becomes the 13-minute walk. Mixing both means accepting a walk on
some days.

**B. Bounty and satellite formats: wanted or not?**
They are 58 of the 80 single-entry events. If they are out, the plan drops to 22 plain freezeouts. The Format
filter on the Single entry page switches between the two views.

**C. How much re-entry risk is acceptable?**
The 28 re-entry events and the 47 multiple-entry events are all playable, and some of them carry the largest
guarantees on the calendar. They are kept in the separate section so they never sit silently inside a
single-entry ranking. If you want them ranked alongside, that is a different table and a different decision.

**D. What should the room budget be?**
Until a dated rate is captured, the only published anchor is the Venetian poker-player rate from $119. Adding
the $55 resort fee plus 13.38% tax makes the fixed nightly cost $62.36 before any room charge. That arithmetic
is shown on the Hotels page, and the room rate itself is deliberately blank.

**E. Should the daily feed be trusted during the series?**
The Wynn daily structure sheets say the dailies may be suspended during other tournament series. If you are
planning around a daily, confirm it with the poker room on the day. The Daily feed page shows the published
format and the suspension language together so this is never invisible.
