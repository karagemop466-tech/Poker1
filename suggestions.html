Suggestions for better decisions

This page keeps enhancement ideas separate from verified findings. Choose which paths make sense before implementing more collection or scoring behavior.

Selection principle: prioritize changes that close material decision gaps, preserve the source trail, and avoid false precision.
01 · highest impactRecommended first

Maintain an official-entry-policy registry

For every event record: entry cap, re-entry allowed, re-entry cutoff, rebuy/add-on policy, multiple-flight rules, age restrictions, and date/time last confirmed. Keep “unknown” distinct from “no.” Contact venue directly where official schedules omit the rule.

Decision gain: makes a genuinely strict single-entry filter possible. Source basis: current official Venetian structures omit explicit entry limits on the reviewed one-day events.

Official series schedule · Official poker room contact

02 · high impactRecommended

Add date-based hotel quote comparisons

Only after arrival/departure dates and occupancy are known, capture room rate, mandatory resort fee, applicable taxes, parking, deposits, cancellation policy, and total stay divided by nights. Save the quote timestamp and official booking URL; recheck before purchase.

Decision gain: compares actual nightly costs instead of headline rates. The two official operator sources publish mandatory $55 + tax resort fees and date-dependent booking information.

Venetian official fee · Wynn official booking details

03 · high impactRecommended

Use a walkability check with route evidence

Record address-to-entrance walking duration from a consistent mapping source, whether it is indoor/on-property, accessible route availability, and last-check time. Include an uncertainty buffer and flag major construction or pedestrian-route changes. Do not use straight-line distance as walking time.

Decision gain: applies the 30-minute threshold in a repeatable and auditable way. Current official sources establish venue addresses and on-property locations but not route minutes.

Venetian room location · Wynn official directions

04 · decision qualityOptional

Replace “best tournament” with transparent filters and trade-offs

Allow user-selected maximum buy-in, minimum official guarantee, eligible game/format, confirmed one-entry policy, age eligibility, and preferred start-time range. Show guarantee/buy-in only as a computed screening ratio, clearly marked not expected value. Let users sort, not hide, the underlying source facts.

Decision gain: separates personal preference from venue facts and avoids presenting a computed ratio as a prediction.

Current candidate analysis · Current decision gates

05 · schedule qualityOptional

Model event conflicts and actual play duration

Store registration close, blind level length, break pattern, scheduled restart, expected finish range (only if venue-published), and overlap. Generate a daily plan with one event at a time and clearly show unfillable hours. Do not promise multi-event play from start times alone.

Decision gain: prevents impossible plans and addresses the 8 AM-to-late goal with the actual calendar.

Official schedule

06 · trust / maintenanceRecommended

Automate source freshness and broken-link checks

For every row, store last-checked date, official/secondary provenance, source status, and a “changed since last review” note. Run scheduled link checks and flag a row rather than retaining stale guarantees or prices silently. Keep official links primary.

Decision gain: tournament schedules, fee pages, and offers can change after this snapshot.
