Single-entry tournaments (verified)

Each row's official structure sheet was read and shows no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or flight clause. Sort by any column; the default sort is pool ÷ entry fee.

How to read this site. Every row links to an official source (venue or operator domain). A cell is blank when the official source does not state that value — nothing is inferred or filled in. Pool ÷ entry fee is a descriptive ratio of the published guarantee to the published buy-in; it is not a forecast of what you will win or of likely field size. Bounty and multi-flight events are labelled separately.

The Venetian structure sheets do not print an explicit "no re-entry" sentence. The single-entry verdict is an absence-of-clause finding, so confirm with the poker room before you rely on it (see question I1).
