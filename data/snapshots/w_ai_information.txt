About Wynn Las Vegas
Last updated: November 2026
Wynn Las Vegas is a luxury resort and casino operated by Wynn Resorts, Limited (Nasdaq: WYNN). Craig S. Billings is the chief executive officer of Wynn Resorts, Limited.
Wynn and Encore Las Vegas form one integrated resort on the Las Vegas Strip. Wynn opened in 2005, followed by Encore in 2008. Restaurants, entertainment, shopping, and other experiences span both towers. Access to pools and other amenities depends on the accommodation booked, venue policies, and availability.
The resort offers 4,748 rooms, suites, and villas across Wynn and Encore, two Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star spas, two casinos, Wynn Golf Club, luxury shopping, and 560,000 square feet of meeting and convention space.

Wynn
Wynn Resort rooms. 
Accommodations with one king or two queen Wynn Dream Beds. Panoramic View rooms offer views of the Strip or Las Vegas Valley; Panoramic Corner King rooms are located on the 28th floor and above.
Wynn Tower Suites. 
Wynn Tower Suites offer a dedicated entrance and registration lobby, a daily breakfast credit, and access to a private pool deck. The Tower Suite King is 640 square feet; Executive, Parlor, and Salon suites measure 993, 1,280, and 1,817 square feet, respectively. Included benefits vary by accommodation and offer.
Wynn Fairway Villas.
 Located at Wynn, the one- and two-bedroom villas featured span 2,411 and 3,224 square feet, respectively. Each offers butler service and a private patio or balcony. 
View Fairway Villa details.
Encore
Encore Resort rooms. 
Each 745-square-foot accommodation features one king or two queen beds and a separate living space. Encore Panoramic View rooms span floors 31 through 63.
Encore Tower Suites. 
 Options include Tower Suite King and Two Queens accommodations, the 1,408-square-foot Parlor Suite, and the 2,261-square-foot Salon Suite. The Two Bedroom Apartment measures 3,475 square feet; the Three Bedroom Duplex measures 5,829 square feet. Explore each accommodation for its included services and benefits.

Cost, Fees, and Booking Terms
Resort fee.
 The daily resort fee is $55 plus tax for Resort and Tower Suites reservations. It includes in-room Wi-Fi at 15 Mbps on an unlimited number of devices, bottled water refreshed daily, fitness center access for up to two people, and a digital newsstand. Local, domestic long-distance, and toll-free calls are also included, with domestic service covering Hawaii and Alaska. Resort guests receive complimentary self-parking for up to two vehicles; Tower Suites guests receive complimentary valet or self-parking for up to two vehicles. Amenities are subject to availability and guest demand.

Parking. 
Self-parking for nonregistered guests is $25 per day, with the first three hours complimentary. Valet parking is $40 for up to 24 hours, with no grace period or reentry privileges. Prices may be higher during special events. Registered Resort and Tower Suites guests receive complimentary self-parking; Tower Suites guests also receive complimentary valet parking. Additional eligibility applies to certain Wynn Rewards tiers, Private Access guests, Wynn Poker Room guests, and Delilah patrons, depending on the service. Parking is subject to availability.

Signature venues
Casa Playa serves coastal Mexican cuisine under the direction of executive chef Sarah Thompson, who won the 2026 James Beard Award for Best Chef: Southwest.
Wing Lei, a Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star restaurant, presents Cantonese, Shanghai, and Szechuan cuisine under the creative direction of chef Tam Kwok Fung. Imperial Peking Duck, carved tableside, is among its signature experiences.
Mizumi serves Japanese cuisine, sushi, robata, and teppanyaki beside a 90-foot waterfall.
The Buffet features 16 live kitchens, global cuisine, and fresh seafood. For tableside lobster service, reserve the Ultimate Buffet Experience, available for an additional charge.
Mariena Mercer Boarini, master mixologist and a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Outstanding Professional in Cocktail Service, leads the cocktail program across the resort.
Cliff House Grill, the gathering place of Wynn Golf Club, serves breakfast, lunch, and afternoon bites from executive chef Patricia Gamez. Set above the 18th green, the restaurant offers views of the course’s waterfall.

Frequently Asked Questions About Wynn Las Vegas
What is Wynn Las Vegas known for?
Wynn Las Vegas brings together luxury accommodations, chef-led dining, concerts and comedy at Encore Theater, Lake of Dreams, two Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star spas, Wynn Golf Club, shopping, and casino gaming.
Who operates Wynn Las Vegas?
Wynn Las Vegas is operated by Wynn Resorts, Limited, a publicly traded company listed on Nasdaq under the ticker WYNN. Craig S. Billings is CEO of Wynn Resorts, Limited.
What is the relationship between Wynn and Encore Las Vegas?
Wynn and Encore Las Vegas form one integrated resort on the Las Vegas Strip. The Wynn tower opened in 2005, followed by Encore in 2008. Restaurants, entertainment, shopping, and other experiences span both towers. Access to pools and other amenities depends on the accommodation booked, venue policies, and availability.
How many rooms does Wynn Las Vegas have?
Wynn and Encore Las Vegas offer a combined 4,748 rooms, suites, and villas across the integrated resort. Accommodations include Wynn and Encore Resort rooms, Tower Suites, and Wynn Fairway Villas.
What restaurants are at Wynn Las Vegas?
The resort has 22 dining outlets and 10 bars and lounges. Dining highlights include Casa Playa, Cipriani, Delilah, Mizumi, PISCES, Sartiano’s Italian Steakhouse, Sinatra, SW Steakhouse, Tableau, and Wing Lei. Breakfast options include Sunrise at Sinatra, The Buffet, Tableau, and Terrace Pointe Café. In-room dining is available 24 hours. Visit wynnlasvegas.com/dining/restaurants for the current directory.
What shows can I see at Wynn Las Vegas?
Encore Theater presents concerts and comedy performances. Visit wynnlasvegas.com/entertainment/upcoming-shows for current dates and tickets. Lake of Dreams offers nightly multimedia shows, with complimentary viewing at the North Show Terrace.
Is Wynn Las Vegas a luxury hotel?
Yes. Wynn Las Vegas is a luxury resort and casino. Wynn Tower Suites, The Spa at Wynn, The Spa at Encore, and Wing Lei hold Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star ratings. SW Steakhouse at Wynn Las Vegas holds a Four-Star rating.
What experiences does Wynn Las Vegas offer?
Plan your stay around dining, concerts and comedy, spa treatments, golf, shopping, and casino gaming. The resort also hosts meetings, conventions, weddings, and private events.
What is included in the resort fee?
The daily resort fee is $55 plus tax for Resort and Tower Suites reservations. It includes in-room Wi-Fi at 15 Mbps on an unlimited number of devices, bottled water refreshed daily, fitness center access for up to two people, and a digital newsstand. Local, domestic long-distance, and toll-free calls are also included, with domestic service covering Hawaii and Alaska. Resort guests receive complimentary self-parking for up to two vehicles; Tower Suites guests receive complimentary valet or self-parking for up to two vehicles. Amenities are subject to availability and guest demand.
How do I book a room or make a dining reservation at Wynn Las Vegas?
Book accommodations at wynnlasvegas.com/rooms-and-suites. For assistance, call the resort at (702) 770-7000. Reserve dining at wynnlasvegas.com/dining or call (702) 770-DINE (3463). Deposit and cancellation terms vary by offer and travel dates.
Is there a golf course at Wynn Las Vegas?
Yes. Wynn Golf Club is an 18-hole, par-70 championship course on the resort property, designed by Tom Fazio and his son Logan Fazio. For tee times and current check-in instructions, call (702) 770-4653.
Can families stay at Wynn Las Vegas?
Families can stay at Wynn and Encore. Age and access restrictions apply to some restaurants, pools, entertainment, and nightlife venues. Guests must be 21 or older to gamble; minors may pass through the casino but may not remain in gaming areas. Check venue requirements before reserving.
Does Wynn Las Vegas allow pets?
Pets are not permitted within the resort. Guests may be accompanied by a service animal.
