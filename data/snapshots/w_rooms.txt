Behind the Design with Todd-Avery Lenahan
Wynn Las Vegas guest rooms and suites are designed in soft, subtle hues, complemented by thoughtfully layered lighting that enhances both comfort and well-being. Every lighting element is tailored to the way guests use their space, whether working, preparing for an evening out, or simply unwinding.
“In addition to ensuring every room and suite is impeccably appointed, this is the space our guests experience most personally,” says Todd-Avery Lenahan, president and chief creative officer of Wynn Design & Development. “It becomes their home when they’re with us. As guest preferences continue to evolve, we’re constantly refining every detail not only to meet but to exceed expectations.”
Throughout each room and suite, carefully considered details are integrated into a design rich in texture, warmth, and scale. “The art, all commissioned for Wynn, is generous in size, while the bed has a really confident presentation,” Lenahan says. “Our guests are quite sophisticated, and they appreciate these and other nuances, all designed with their comfort in mind.”

Frequently Asked Questions
What amenities are included in my room purchase?
As a part of booking your stay at Wynn Las Vegas, you'll receive:
Complimentary self-parking for up to two vehicles;
In-room Wi-Fi of 15 Mbps on an unlimited number of devices;
Complimentary bottled water, refreshed daily;
Unlimited local, domestic long distance (including HI and AK) and toll free calls;
Access for up to two persons to our fitness center; and
A digital newsstand of magazines and newspapers.
Some amenities, such as the fitness center, parking, and pool, are subject to availability and guest demand.
When can I check in to my room?
You may check in to your room at 3 p.m.
When do I need to check out of my room?
Checkout time is at 12 p.m. Please call the Front Desk at 
(702) 770-2150
 if you need to check out late, as late checkout is based on availability.
Is smoking allowed in my room?
Smoking is allowed on the Casino floor, outside in the patio and pool areas, and on designated hotel floors. For designated non-smoking rooms, there is a cleaning fee of up to $1,500 charged for smoking. Marijuana products are prohibited in guest rooms and throughout the resort.

What is the Wynn Rate Promise?
The Wynn and Encore Las Vegas promise to you: The rates offered on our website are the lowest available anywhere online, guaranteed. In the unlikely event that you do find a better rate elsewhere, we promise to match it and upgrade your room.

To check out through the app, select “Check Out.” You will be provided a summary with billing details and charges, and must confirm your email to receive a finalized report. From there, click “Check Out” and once processed, a confirmation message will appear.

Does Wynn or Encore Las Vegas offer complimentary parking?
The daily self-parking fee for non-registered guests at Wynn and Encore Las Vegas is $25, with the first three hours free. Valet parking is $40 per day.
Complimentary self-parking is extended to registered Resort and Tower Suite guests, Wynn Poker Room guests, Private Access members and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman, Black and Platinum tiers.
Complimentary valet parking is available to guests staying in Wynn and Encore Tower Suites, Private Access guests, Delilah patrons and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman and Black Tiers.
