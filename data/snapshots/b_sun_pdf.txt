1. The $400 Buy-In is allocated as follows: $340 to Prize Pool; $35 
House Fee; $25 Voluntary Staff Gratuity. 
2. BELLAGIO Poker does not withhold any percentage of the prize 
pool for Staff gratuities. A voluntarty Staff gratuity of $25, 
at registration only, will  grant players an additional 15,000 in 
starting chips. This is an optional gratuity that will be provided 
directly to the Staff and will not be kept by the House.  
   
3. Players will start with 30,000 tournament chips, including the 
optional Staff Gratuity. (15,000 + 15,000)  
4. Each level will last thirty (30) minutes, game will change every 
8 hands regardless of how many players are seated at table.  
***When 3 players remain in event, levels will be reduced to 20 
Minutes. 6 hands of each game will be played. When 2 players 
remain, 4 hands of each game will be played.  
 
 
 
5. Players may buy-in and re-enter through the end of level 8 
(Including Break).  Tournament Surrender is allowed on Level 8 
break.     
6. Twelve (12) minute breaks will be every 4 levels. 
7. Payout structure based on number of entries and will be 
approximately 12.5% of field.    
8. Tournament will be played 8 handed. Final table will be 9 
handed.     
9. Management reserves the right to cancel or change tournaments.  
Tournaments will use TDA rules.
10. Residents of foreign countries without a U.S. tax treaty will be 
subject to withholding.    
11. All players must use their Player’s Club Card to enter the 
tournament.     
12. This tournament is subject to NRS 463.362.
