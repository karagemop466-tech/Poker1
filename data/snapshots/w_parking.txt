Parking Information
Self-Parking
Self-parking garages at Wynn and Encore are conveniently located at each resort just off Las Vegas Boulevard with complimentary parking for all Wynn and Encore Resort and Tower Suite registered guests, subject to availability. 
The daily parking fee for non-registered guests is $25 per day with the first three (3) hours complimentary. Prices may be higher during special events. 
Complimentary self-parking is extended to Wynn Poker Room guests (please visit the Poker Room for details), Private Access members, and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman, Black, and Platinum Tiers.
Valet Parking
Valet parking is $40 per day up to 24 hours (no grace period, no re-entry). Non-registered guests are charged the applicable daily fee each time they use valet parking and have no re-entry privileges. Prices may be higher during special events.
Valet parking is complimentary for: Wynn and Encore Tower Suite registered guests, Private Access guests, Delilah patrons and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman and Black Tiers.
All valet parking is subject to availability.
Check-In
For hotel guests, scan your valid hotel room key at either the entry or exit gate.
Frequently Asked Questions
DOES WYNN OR ENCORE LAS VEGAS OFFER COMPLIMENTARY PARKING?
All Wynn and Encore registered guests with booked Resort or Tower Suite accommodations receive complimentary self-parking during their stay. Tower Suites registered guests are also eligible for complimentary valet parking during their stay.
Complimentary self-parking is also extended to Wynn Poker Room guests, Private Access members and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman, Black and Platinum Tiers.
Does Wynn offer Valet Services? 
Valet parking is $40 per day for up to 24 hours (no grace period, no re-entry). Prices may be higher during special events.
Valet parking is complimentary for: Wynn and Encore Tower Suites registered guests, Private Access guests, Delilah patrons and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman and Black Tiers.
HOW IS COMPLIMENTARY PARKING REDEEMED?
If eligible for complimentary parking, registered guests should adhere to the following instructions:
Registered Resort and Tower Suite guests must insert their room key at the exit gate.
Private Access members, along with Wynn Rewards members who qualify for the Chairman, Black and Platinum tiers, must insert their membership card at the exit gate.
Poker guests must have their parking ticket verified at the Wynn Poker Room (visit the Poker Room for details). This ticket must be scanned at the exit gate.
What is the fee for valet parking?
Valet parking is $40 per day up to 24 hours (no grace period, no re-entry). Non-registered guests are charged the applicable daily fee each time they use valet parking and have no re-entry privileges. Valet parking is complimentary for: Wynn and Encore Tower Suite registered guests, Private Access guests, Delilah patrons and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman and Black Tiers. Prices may be higher during special events.
Where are the Self-parking garages located? 
Self-park garages are located just off Las Vegas Blvd. for both Wynn Las Vegas and Encore.
For Wynn Las Vegas self parking, head north on Las Vegas Blvd. Turn right just after passing the Wynn Main Gate. For the Encore self-parking garage, head north on Las Vegas Blvd. Turn right on Encore Resort Blvd. Follow the access road to the back of the property to find the self-park garage.
Is Rideshare available?
Rideshare services pick up and drop off at all Wynn Las Vegas and Encore Resort entrances.
What are the current parking policies for Wynn and Encore Las Vegas?
Current self-parking and valet parking policies are outlined above.
Wynn and Encore Las Vegas have reinstated self-parking fees for non-resort guests. The daily parking fee is $25 per day, with the first three (3) hours complimentary. Prices may be higher during special events. Valet parking is $40 per day.
Complimentary self-parking is extended to registered Resort and Tower Suite guests, Wynn Poker Room guests, Private Access members, and Wynn Rewards members in the Chairman, Platinum and Black tiers. Complimentary valet parking is available to guests staying in Wynn and Encore Tower Suites, as well as Private Access members, guests of Delilah and Wynn Rewards members in the Black and Chairman tiers.
