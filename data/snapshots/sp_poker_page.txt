Poker Room at South Point Hotel & Casino
South Point Hotel and Casino has a 24-hour non-smoking poker room. South Point Poker Room offers all of the most popular poker games and a variety of betting limits. Games featured are $4-8, $8-16 Limit Texas Hold'em, $4-$8, $8-16 Omaha 8/B, and $1-2, $2-3, $3-5 blind No-Limit Texas Hold'em games. Mixed Games, Stud and Omaha spread upon request. 
We feature tournaments daily with aggressive guarantees at 10:10 am, 2:10 pm, 6:10 pm and 10:10 pm with buy-ins of $120 and $300.
Room rates are available with 4 hours of cash play for our out-of-town guests and parking is always free!
With all of the above, along with the service provided by our courteous, professional staff, we are certain you'll find your poker playing experience an enjoyable one!
