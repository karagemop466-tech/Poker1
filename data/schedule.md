# All-Day Tournament Schedule — Single Entry Focus

**Research date:** 2026-11-16. All times from official sources (Venetian DSE IV schedule, Wynn WPT schedule).

## Key constraint: nothing starts at 8 AM

The earliest official tournament start on the Strip during this window is **10:10 AM** (Venetian RaceDay / Seniors events). No official source lists an 8 AM tournament start. An 8 AM start is therefore **not available** — the schedule below starts at the first official event of the day. Flagged: verify with poker rooms if earlier events are added.

## How to build your own all-day schedule

1. Pick a date from Section A (single-entry 1Day events).
2. Morning block: 10:10 AM or 11:10 AM event.
3. Evening block: 5:10 PM bounty event (only if morning event is done — they can overlap; 1Day events play until a winner).
4. Walk from Venetian/Palazzo: 0 min. From Wynn/Encore: ~12–15 min (estimate).
5. Budget: add buy-ins; no add-ons on single-entry list; re-entry events are in Section B only.
