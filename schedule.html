3Play-All-Day Tournament Schedule

Goal: enter as many live tournaments as possible on a planned vacation, playing all day. Anchor venue: Wynn Las Vegas poker room (WPT World Championship Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026). All times Pacific. All events listed are from the official Wynn schedule; entry type is as verified on the Tournaments page.

No verified 8 a.m. start exists

The earliest tournament on the current verified Wynn schedule starts at 10 a.m. or 11 a.m. (WPT series Day 1 flights) or 12 p.m. (Wynn daily tournaments). There is no verified 8 a.m. single-entry tournament on the Strip schedule. The plan below starts at the first available event and runs through the evening — this is the closest achievable version of "play all day."

Mixing events across the day is possible only where flights/times don't overlap; Day 1 flights (10 a.m., 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 5 p.m.) run concurrently across different events, so you can realistically play one main tournament plus possibly a late-night turbo in a single day.

Why a full 8 a.m. — close schedule isn't achievable yet

  • No 8 a.m. event: the first verified tournament starts at 10–11 a.m. (WPT flights) or 12 p.m. (Wynn daily).
  • No single-entry events confirmed: all verified events allow re-entry/rebuy/add-on.
  • Overlapping flights: multiple Day 1 flights run at the same time, so you cannot play them all in one day.

Suggested next steps to build a real schedule

  • Call the Wynn poker room ((702) 770-7654) or visit wynnpoker.com to confirm the daily schedule and any single-entry/freezeout events for your travel dates.
  • Check other Strip venues' official poker pages (Venetian, Caesars, Bellagio, Horseshoe, Resorts World) for morning single-entry events.
  • Monitor for new series announcements (Wynn Signature Series begins January 11–24, 2027).
