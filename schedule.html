How to build your own all-day schedule

  1. Pick a date from the single-entry list (tournaments page).
  2. Morning block: 10:10 AM or 11:10 AM event.
  3. Evening block: 5:10 PM bounty event — only if your morning event is finished; 1-Day events can overlap, and you cannot play two at once.
  4. Walk from Venetian/Palazzo: 0 min. From Wynn/Encore: ~12–15 min (UNVERIFIED estimate).
  5. Add up buy-ins. No add-ons on the single-entry list; re-entry events belong in Section B.
