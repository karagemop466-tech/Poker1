# Las Vegas Strip poker-tournament research

**Checked:** 2026-11-16 (Las Vegas local time, Pacific Time)  
**Scope:** Publicly posted live poker schedules at the Strip and immediately adjacent resorts. The published information currently available spans recurring room schedules and specific series from 2026-11-16 through 2027-01-10. It is not a guarantee that every listed recurring event will run on every date; casinos reserve the right to change or cancel events.  
**Source rule:** Populated event facts below come only from official resort, tournament-operator, or official structure-sheet pages. Unpublished values are blank in `data/tournaments.json`; official links are supplied with each record.

## Executive finding

**No tournament in the checked, currently published official schedules could be certified as both (a) single-entry with no re-entry/rebuy/add-on, and (b) free of any paid chip advantage.** The strict qualifying list is therefore empty. This is a scoped finding, not proof that no such tournament exists anywhere in Las Vegas: several rooms do not publish their current entry rules, and several posted tournaments explicitly allow additional entries or paid extras.

The high-guarantee candidates are not strict single-entry recommendations:

- The **$400 WPT Super Gold Million** at Wynn has a **$3,000,000 guarantee** and **unlimited re-entry** (Dec 10–14). Its guarantee-to-buy-in ratio is 7,500:1, but it expressly fails the entry rule.
- The **$1,100 WPT Prime Championship** at Wynn has a **$5,000,000 guarantee** and permits **one entry plus one re-entry per starting flight** (Dec 5–12). It also fails the entry rule.
- The **$800 Venetian No-Limit Hold'em** event has a **$500,000 guarantee** (Dec 3–6). The structure sheet prevents a player who bags for Day 2 from entering a later starting flight, but does not explicitly establish the allowed re-entry count before bagging; it cannot be certified as single-entry.
- The **$600 Wynn No-Limit Hold'em** event has a **$400,000 guarantee** and unlimited re-entry per starting flight (Nov 27–30).

The guarantee/buy-in ratios are descriptive comparisons only—not expected value, return, or probability of cashing. Bounty guarantees include bounty prizes where the official structure says so. “Single day” and “late registration” do not, by themselves, prove single-entry.

## Schedule feasibility

- No checked official schedule posts an 8:00 a.m. tournament start. The earliest recurring time found is **9:00 a.m. at Horseshoe Las Vegas**, as stated in a Caesars resort article; it does not publish the exact buy-in, prize guarantee, or entry/re-entry terms there. The Venetian lists 10:10 a.m. starts; Caesars Palace lists 10:00 a.m.; MGM Grand lists 11:00 a.m.; Wynn's published regular weekday event starts at noon; the WPT Wynn festival starts as early as 10:00 a.m. for Super Gold Million and 11:00 a.m. for other events.
- The Caesars article says many tournament sessions last four to six hours; that is a general estimate, not a promised finish time. Tournament finish times depend on the field and play. Therefore, a same-day chain of tournaments cannot be promised from start times alone.
- **Do not schedule overlapping events as if both are playable.** For an 8:00 a.m. “start,” use that as a check-in/registration/breakfast buffer, then choose a published start time. A second event should be treated as conditional on busting early or receiving a confirmed finish-time estimate from the room.
- Wynn states its regular daily schedule is suspended during published series. The WPT festival is Nov 27–Dec 21, so do not plan Wynn's regular daily events during those dates.

## Decision questions to resolve before selecting a tournament

These are information gaps, not questions for the user to answer in this session. The site should retain them as a checklist and use official contacts/schedules to resolve them.

1. **Is re-entry allowed, and how many times?** Ask whether this is one entry per player, one per flight, unlimited, or capped; ask whether the player may enter a second flight after advancing.
2. **Are rebuys or add-ons offered?** Ask the exact cost, chip amount, eligibility, and time limit. Confirm if a dealer gratuity or optional staff contribution adds chips.
3. **What does the posted guarantee include?** Separate prize pool from bounty pool, satellites, added money, fees and any conditional guarantee. Use only the published guarantee, not total series guarantees.
4. **What is the complete buy-in outlay?** Confirm tournament fee, staff service charge, promo discount eligibility, membership cost, rake discount and required rewards-card membership. Do not combine a discounted rate with a regular rate without stating the conditions.
5. **Is this schedule valid for the actual date?** Confirm no event-series suspension, schedule change, sold-out field, age restriction, or limited capacity.
6. **When does registration open/close, and how long can play run?** Late registration is not a separate entry; it does not prove a freezeout. Confirm the entry cutoff and expected duration before attempting another event.
7. **Can two starts actually be played in one day?** Ask the event director for expected time to completion, not just the clock start. Do not count same-day tournaments that overlap or depend on busting early.
8. **What are eligibility limits?** Confirm age restrictions (for example seniors), game variant, ID/rewards-card requirements and whether the event is invitation-only.
