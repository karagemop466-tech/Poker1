### Correction carried out in the integration pass

Session `673f9643` reported that DSE IV sheet 18 starts the RaceDay events with **30,000** chips and a
"20/15 minutes" level length. The first transcription of that sheet had recorded 25,000 chips and a flat
20-minute level. The sheet was re-read on 2026-11-16 and session `673f9643` was right: the sheet prints
**Starting Chips: 30,000**, **Level Duration: 20/15 minutes** (levels 1-6 at 20 minutes, levels 7-28 at
15 minutes) and **Late Registration: 6 levels + Break (~ 12:25 PM)**. The generator and this log now use the
corrected values.

Sheets 1, 2 and 18 were re-read in the same pass and matched the values recorded here, so the starting chip
count published in `data/tournaments.json` is limited to the sheets re-confirmed on 2026-11-16 (November
sheets 1, 2, 18, 19, 24 and 25 and December sheets 4, 5 and 7) plus every Wynn structure published in the
official WPT structures PDF. The remaining chip counts in the tables below come from the first direct read
of each sheet and are recorded for reference only.

## Official event-page rows (the dates/times/guarantees used in the dataset)

Source: `dse-nov-2026.html` (DeepStack Extravaganza IV, Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026) and
`dse-dec-2026.html` (DeepStack Extravaganza NYE, Dec 24, 2026 – Jan 10, 2027).
Both pages were read in full on 2026-11-16. Every row links to its structure sheet; the link
printed on the page is the one stored as the row's structure source.

Date irregularities found on the official pages (kept, not silently corrected):
- `dse-dec-2026.html` prints the Dec 26 rows as "Saturday, December 26, **2025**" although the
  series header reads "December 24, 2026 – January 10, 2027". Rows are stored as 2026-12-26 with
  the printed date string kept in the row and the discrepancy flagged on the Flagged page.
- `dse-dec-2026.html` prints "Tuesaday" for the Jan 5, 2027 rows (typo in the official page).
- `dse-nov-2026.html` prints "No LImit" (capital I) for some event names.
- `WPT_World_Championship_Schedule.pdf` prints one block under the heading "Dec. 18 Wednesday".
  December 18, 2026 is a Friday and the PDF prints a separate "Dec. 18 Friday" block, so the
  "Dec. 18 Wednesday" rows are stored as **December 16, 2026**, which is also the only reading that
  fits the WPT World Championship day order (Day 1A Dec 13, 1B Dec 14, 1C Dec 15, Day 2 Dec 16).
- `WPT_World_Championship_Schedule.pdf` prints the $600 NLH Turbo on Dec 21 at 8 p.m., while the
  $600 NLH Turbo structure sheet lists "Monday, December 21, 2026 at 6 p.m." The schedule time is
  kept and the conflict is flagged on the row.
- `WPT_World_Championship_Schedule.pdf` prints a buy-in in the "Day 2" rows that belongs to a
  different event than the day it names (for example "Dec. 3 · 1 p.m. · No Limit Hold'em Day 2"
  carries $1,600 but the $800 structure sheet prints that Day 2). The structure sheet is treated
  as authoritative for the day-to-event link and the printed value is flagged on the row.
