# Structure sheets read (official sources)

Every row in this file was produced by opening the official structure-sheet PDF and reading it.
Retrieval date for all entries: **2026-11-16**.

Fields captured per sheet:

- `events` — event numbers the sheet applies to
- `buy_in` — buy-in printed on the sheet
- `fee` — house fee (rake + staff) withheld from the buy-in, as printed
- `start` — start time printed on the sheet
- `chips` — starting chips
- `level_min` — level duration in minutes
- `late_reg` — late-registration cutoff printed on the sheet
- `guarantee` — guarantee printed on the sheet
- `single_entry` — `yes` only when the sheet contains **no** re-entry, rebuy, add-on, or
  multi-flight clause. `no` when such a clause is present. `unclear` when the sheet wording
  leaves it open.
- `excerpt` — verbatim text copied from the sheet

## Walking times

No resort publishes a hotel-to-poker-room walking time, and the official "getting here" pages for the
Venetian and Wynn do not state one. Walking times in this project are therefore **computed**, not quoted:
a single OpenStreetMap/OSRM pedestrian-routing table request over the official street addresses above.

Source (reproducible, one request returns the whole matrix):
`https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/table/v1/driving/-115.1693352,36.1217073;-115.1682710,36.1241843;-115.1658180,36.1263781;-115.1653620,36.1289345;-115.1787230,36.1123192;-115.1779977,36.1070287;-115.1751896,36.1167079?annotations=duration,distance`

Coordinates come from OpenStreetMap Nominatim lookups of each official street address. Every hotel row
also carries a Google Maps and an Apple Maps walking-directions link so a reader can confirm the figure
on their own map application before booking.
