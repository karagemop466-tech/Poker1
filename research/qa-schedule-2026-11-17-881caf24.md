## Method and interpretation

`scripts/check_schedule_plan.py` recomputes absolute start minutes from each row's ISO date and `HH:MM` start, then adds its stored `est_duration_min`. It compares those intervals, and exact same-start pairs, with the primary/fallback and conflict pairs in `data/schedule.json`. A 15-hour-45-minute estimate beginning at 11:10 a.m. ends at 2:55 a.m. the **next day**, not 2:55 a.m. on the event's start date.

Structure-derived finish times are **estimates, not operator-published or actual finish times**. A duration-based intersection is a planning warning, not proof of the real finish time. Two events scheduled for the same start time are a known start conflict even if their finish durations are unknown. When either event has no usable start/duration, its end is indeterminate; it is not treated as conflict-free.
