## 3. DEFECT — estimated end times wrap past midnight; the day plan inverts (logged, not edited)

`data/tournaments.json` stores `est_end` as a **time-only string** (`"08:55"`) with no date,
while `est_duration_min` holds the true duration. When start + duration crosses midnight the
stored time is the *next morning*, but nothing records that. Three consequences on `main`:

1. **Display reads as impossible.** `assets/app.js` renders
   `"estimated to finish about " + timeFmt(est_end)` — e.g. the Nov 16, 11:10 AM flight shows
   "estimated to finish about **8:55 AM**" (it means 8:55 AM the next day). **137 of 317 rows**
   have an `est_end` that wraps or reads before their start.
2. **All 24 conditional fallbacks are impossible sequences.** `scripts/build_data.py` selects a
   fallback with `to_min(cand.start) >= to_min(primary.est_end)`. Once the primary wraps,
   `est_end` is a small morning minute count, so *any* same-day candidate passes. Checked
   against `start + est_duration_min`: **24 of the 24 fallback days propose a candidate that
   starts while the primary is still running** (e.g. 2026-11-25: primary r016 11:10 → 02:55
   next day, fallback r017 17:10 same day).
3. **Conflict flags are almost all missed.** The conflict test
   `to_min(b.start) < to_min(a.est_end)` fails for every wrapped `a`. Recomputing with
   absolute minutes (`a.start + a.est_duration_min`) against the published `schedule.json`:
   **14 published conflicts are correct, 231 real conflicts are missing, 0 spurious.**

Not edited here because `scripts/build_data.py` and `assets/app.js` are inside the claimed
file scope of six open PRs (#36, #46, #52, #53, #60, #64). The request and the proposed fix
are logged in `COORDINATION.md` under session 206debc0.

**Proposed fix (for the owning session):**

- Compare in absolute minutes everywhere: `a_end = to_min(a.start) + a.est_duration_min`;
  fallback requires `to_min(cand.start) >= a_end` (and if `a_end >= 1440`, no same-day
  fallback exists); conflict when `to_min(b.start) < a_end` and `b` starts at or after `a.start`.
- Render `est_end` with a " (next day)" suffix when `est_end < start` (for durations under
  24 h this detects every wrap), or publish `est_end` as a full datetime.
- Re-run `build_data.py`, `build_site.py`, `check_links.py` and the smoke test; the fallback
  notes and conflict counts in `data/schedule.json` will change — that is the point.

Open sub-question for the owners: whether the estimates themselves are right for multi-flight
events (r001's flight carries a 1,305-minute estimate). The site already labels them as
estimates; the wrap handling above is required regardless.

## 5. Defect found on `main` — weekly provenance check refuses to run (logged, not edited)

`data/monitor_last_run.txt` on `main` reads:

```
Refusing to run: non-official host(s) in manifest: [('strip_rwlv_events', 'www.rwlasvegas.com')]
```

`scripts/check_source_facts.py` raises `SystemExit` because `www.rwlasvegas.com`
(`strip_rwlv_events`, added by the strip-room survey) is not in its approved-host set, so the
weekly provenance refresh has not actually run since that source landed. The workflow still
reports **success** because the step pipes the script through `tee`, and without `pipefail`
the pipeline exits with `tee`'s status — the refusal is recorded but not failed.

Not edited here: `scripts/check_source_facts.py` and `data/sources.json` are in the claimed
scope of open PRs #53, #59 and #64; PR #64's diff already carries `www.rwlasvegas.com` in its
approved-host set, so it appears to hold the fix. Logged in `COORDINATION.md`.
