## Planning, hotel and remaining research risks

- A printed blind schedule does **not** establish an actual finish time or a hard maximum duration. On the contemporaneous main snapshot (`a0037c5`), the November 26 day plan treats the table end as a bound and excludes a later candidate. A player may bust earlier; a tournament may also continue longer. Treat another event as a conditional option only after finishing or busting, allowing travel, and registering within the actual deadline. The same caution applies across midnight and to satellites.
- Existing rate observations are historical: for example, a Palazzo Luxury King quote on November 26–29 is not a competing same-date quote for the January 10–15 Palazzo Two Queen room. Compare matched dates, occupancy, room class and cancellation/fee terms; do not label different windows like-for-like. The calculator's separate same-window comparison added on `main` is a useful improvement, but any unknown parking and optional charges must remain unknown rather than becoming zero. An event count is not proof that the itinerary can be played.
- The archived walking matrix is a route estimate between resort coordinates, not a measured guest-room-to-seat journey. Several **internal** routes have no time but were marked `within_30_min: true` on the contemporaneous main snapshot. Interior location is not proof of a walk of 30 minutes or less; keep that field unknown until timed.
- [Wynn's official poker page](https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/casino/poker) names Signature, Millions and Classic series in its daily-tournament suspension notice. The wording inspected did not explicitly name WPT; do not silently extend that particular notice to WPT without further evidence.
- [Westgate's official poker page](https://www.westgateresorts.com/hotels/nevada/las-vegas/westgate-las-vegas-resort-casino/casino/poker/), read on 19 November, confirms daily tournaments and telephone **(702) 732-5223**. It does not establish the time, price or freezeout terms of the rumored $70 event. Call or obtain the official event-specific structure rather than promoting third-party listings as confirmed.
- The Orleans schedule PDF's filename refers to October 2026, while its readable grid does not itself print a validity period. Its being linked from a current page does not confirm that the weekly grid applies to a November trip; ask the room before relying on it.
- The county licensing table in `data/snapshots/cc_tle_guide.txt` identifies the Venetian/Palazzo record as `1003089.RES-001` (line 1088), while `main` at `1e7da3a` transposes it as `1000389.RES-001`. The former matches the correction in [PR #98](https://github.com/karagemop466-tech/Poker1/pull/98); preserve that correction during integration. The table is dated April 2021: it records the historic class and rate and does not itself verify every property's current 2026 tax. For Venetian/Palazzo, separately read operator terms print 13.38%; for the other properties, verify current terms or a dated checkout quote rather than upgrading the old table to current fact.
