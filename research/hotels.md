## Hotel-specific findings

### Hilton at Resorts World

- **Four nights, Dec 12–16:** Hilton Deluxe Room, one king, two guests. Nightly room amounts shown were $399.00, $129.00, $198.75, and $198.75. The booking engine itemized $925.50 room subtotal, $123.83 room tax, $220.00 resort fees, and $29.44 resort-fee tax, for a **$1,298.77 displayed total**.
- The standard resort fee is $55 per night plus tax. The quote itemizes it separately.
- **Nine nights, Dec 12–21:** the same room type returned an official itemized total of **$2,302.76**: $1,536.00 room subtotal, $205.52 room tax, $495.00 resort fees, and $66.24 resort-fee tax.
- A Resorts World “All Resort No Fees” offer advertises waived resort fees on qualifying stays through Dec 28, 2026, subject to availability and blackout dates. The offer was tested for Dec 12–16 but was unavailable; it is **not** applied to either total. [Offer terms](https://www.rwlasvegas.com/offers/all-resort-no-fees/) · [Resort World FAQ / fee](https://www.rwlasvegas.com/faq/)
- Cancellation/payment terms were not captured for the selected `DBF26` rate. Inspect the rate-specific terms before booking.

### Wynn Las Vegas

- For **Dec 12–16**, the date-specific room details displayed $434 average/night for “Your Fall to Have It All” and $529 average/night for Flexible Getaway. Both are marked as including the resort fee and excluding taxes.
- **Illustrative only:** 4 × $434 = $1,736 before tax; applying 13.38% yields **$1,968.28**. 4 × $529 = $2,116 before tax; applying 13.38% yields **$2,399.12**. The displayed nightly averages may be rounded; neither arithmetic result is a confirmed checkout total.
- For **Dec 12–21**, Wynn displayed $364 average/night for “Your Fall to Have It All” and $442 average/night for Flexible Getaway. Applying 13.38% to nine times those averages produces illustrative totals of **$3,714.33** and **$4,510.26**.
- Wynn states its resort fee is **$55 per night plus tax**; it is already included in the quoted average rates above, so do not add it a second time. [Official resort information](https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/about-us/resort-information)
- The Fall offer states it must be booked by **Nov 24, 2026**, covers eligible Resort King/Two Queens stays, and has a one-night room-rate-and-tax deposit; cancellation at least 72 hours before arrival avoids a one-night room-rate-and-tax penalty. Flexible Getaway also showed a one-night deposit and a 72-hour cancellation threshold. Verify the exact rate and terms in checkout. [Fall-offer terms](https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/terms-and-conditions/2026-seasonal-fall-offer-public) · [Date-specific room details](https://reservations.wynnlasvegas.com/ibe/details.aspx?hotelID=13763&langID=1&rmID=68658&promo=&voucher=&currID=0&checkIn=12%2F12%2F2026&checkOut=12%2F16%2F2026)

### The Venetian Resort / The Palazzo

- The Dec 12–16 direct booking query returned `$XXX` placeholders and an inconsistent four-plus guest/capacity state, not a valid two-guest quote. This is **not** evidence that the hotel is sold out.
- The official 2026 poker suite page advertises rates “as low as” **$119 and $209/night**, subject to availability and blackout dates. These are starting floors, not a dated rate for this comparison. The rate must be requested by calling the resort and providing code `EPOKR25` and a Venetian Rewards account number.
- To qualify, listed guests must collectively play **six hours of active live cash poker or buy into a $300+ tournament for each night stayed**, before 2 a.m. on the last night. Nights that do not meet the condition are repriced to the prevailing rate. The poker rate cannot be combined with another promotion.
- The resort fee is **$55/night plus applicable tax**. Applying the 13.38% Clark County Primary Gaming Corridor rate to the hypothetical floor plus fees gives illustrative four-night totals of **$789.12** at $119/night or **$1,197.29** at $209/night. For nine nights, the same floor arithmetic gives **$1,775.53** or **$2,693.91**. These figures are **not quotes, confirmed availability, or guaranteed totals**.
- Venetian hotel terms also state a $150/night incidental deposit for suite charging privileges for non-Signature Suites and a $100 plus tax early-departure charge. These are not added to the hypothetical stay totals. [Poker rate and play terms](https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/resort/casino/poker/suite-rates.html) · [Hotel terms, tax and resort fee](https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/legal/hotel-terms-and-conditions.html) · [Clark County lodging-tax document](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:3a73546d-c587-409d-9728-cbcf3a72ed04/original/as/pubhear-roomtax-ord-sb1d2.pdf)

## Source and estimate rules

- **Quoted / itemized total:** use only when a direct hotel booking page returned a date-, guest-, and room-specific stay total with fees/taxes identified (Resorts World). Treasure Island returned date-specific totals in EUR, which remain in EUR.
- **Quoted nightly average:** Wynn displayed a date-specific average with resort fee included and tax excluded. The four-/nine-night arithmetic and tax are shown separately as **estimates**, not official totals.
- **Advertised floor:** Venetian's poker rates are conditional “as low as” prices. Hypothetical floors plus fee/tax are estimates; they do not establish availability.
- **No quote:** Venetian's placeholders and Caesars' calendar error are recorded as failures to obtain a rate, not as a sold-out result.
- The Clark County official lodging-tax document states **13.38%** for resort hotels in the Primary Gaming Corridor. It is used only for clearly labeled illustrations.
