## Open values

- Date-specific nightly room rates are verified for one stay only.
- Bellagio, ARIA and Caesars Palace resort fees are not verified from property-specific official terms.
- Property-specific tax values are left blank unless an official property source confirms them. The Venetian's
  13.38% Clark County room-tax statement is not copied to any other hotel.
- Official walking times to the tournament venues are not published in the reviewed sources. Every walking record
  is therefore blank and carries a walking-route link instead; the 30-minute rule applies to a value only once
  one exists.

## True-cost method

`data/hotels.json` only computes a nightly total if a room rate, a resort fee and a property-specific tax rate
are all verified for the same property. No row has all three, so all per-hotel true-cost cells are blank. The
calculator on the site uses the same rule: it refuses to total anything that depends on an unverified figure and
names the missing value instead.
