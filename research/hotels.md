## True-cost method

`data/hotels.json` only computes a nightly total if a date-specific room rate, resort fee, and property-specific tax rate are all verified. No current row has all required inputs, so all true-cost cells are blank. The Venetian terms confirm the resort fee is plus applicable tax, but that does not supply a room quote or justify assigning the Venetian's tax value to other properties.
