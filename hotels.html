TRUE NIGHTLY PRICE

What must be counted

Room quote

Capture the nightly base rate for every date in the stay, not just an average or “from” price. Room rates can vary by date and room type; the official Venetian poker rate page says rates are subject to occupancy and future rates are available only up to 150 days ahead.

Venetian official rate conditions

Mandatory nightly fee

Venetian / Palazzo: $55 plus tax per night. Wynn / Encore: Wynn’s official booking details also state $55 plus tax per room night. Resort fee is separate from the room rate; no after-tax fee total is calculated here without a confirmed tax/booking quote.

Venetian · Wynn

Conditional poker discount

Venetian’s special poker rate requires, for each night stayed, six hours of active live cash play or buying into a tournament of $300 or more. If the requirement is missed, the nightly rate adjusts to the prevailing rate. This is a material trade-off, not a guaranteed cheap rate; do not play or buy in solely to unlock a hotel discount without comparing total cost.
