Priority ranking (suggestion only)

Cost and quality are higher priority than distance, but distance must be reasonable (≤30 min walk). Quality assessments are subjective and UNVERIFIED; cost data is incomplete (room rates unverified), so no definitive ranking can be made yet.

  1. The Venetian / The Palazzo — on site at the tournament venue (zero walk), verified $55 resort fee, official poker discounted suite rate (play requirement applies). Highest priority if you can meet the play requirement or absorb the rate.
  2. Wynn / Encore — luxury product, verified $45 resort fee, ~12–15 min walk, host of the WPT series. Room rate unverified.
  3. Harrah’s / The LINQ / Caesars Palace — mid-range to upscale, 10–22 min walk, resort fees ~$50–$55 (UNVERIFIED).
  4. Bellagio / ARIA / Cosmopolitan — luxury, 24–30 min walk (borderline), resort fees unverified.
Action before booking: call each hotel for the exact room rate + resort fee + tax for your dates, then compute true cost per night with the formula above. Ask whether the resort fee is taxed and whether self-parking is free for guests.
