Trip at a glance

  • Research date: 2026-11-16. Stay window: Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026 (Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV) and Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026 (Wynn WPT World Championship).
  • Main venue: The Venetian Poker Room — > $3.6 million in guaranteed prize pools across the series ([official](https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/resort/casino/poker/deepstack-extravaganza-poker-tournament.html)).
  • Second venue: Wynn Las Vegas — WPT World Championship, including a $10,400 Main Event and a $400 Super Gold Million with $3,000,000 guarantee ([official schedule](https://cdn.wynnresorts.com/image/upload/v1757097329/visitwynn_pdfs_files/Poker/WPT/WPT_World_Championship_Schedule.pdf)).
  • Single entry only: the main tournament list covers Venetian 1-Day events ($200–$1,100 buy-ins). Re-entry / rebuy / add-on / multi-flight events are in Section B and excluded from the single-entry plan.
  • Hotel: stay at the Venetian/Palazzo if possible (on site, zero walk; verified $55 resort fee + tax; poker discounted suite rate from $119/$209 with a play requirement). Otherwise Wynn/Encore (~12–15 min walk estimate, unverified; verified $45 resort fee). All other hotels are 10–30 min walk estimates with unverified rates and fees.
  • Schedule: no official 8 AM start exists anywhere — earliest is 10:10 AM. Build days around an 11:10 AM event plus a 5:10 PM bounty event.
