B. Information we already have (verified)

  • WPT World Championship at Wynn Las Vegas runs Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026 (official Wynn site + schedule PDF).
  • Every WPT event reviewed allows re-entry (unlimited or one re-entry per starting flight) — none are single entry.
  • All Wynn daily tournaments allow re-entry and/or add-on.
  • Wynn resort fee: $55 + tax/night; free guest self-parking; valet $40/day (hotel-policy sources).
  • Venetian/Palazzo resort fee: $55 + tax/night; self-parking $20/day for hotel guests.
  • Clark County lodging tax: 13.38%.

C. Information we do NOT have

  • Any confirmed single-entry tournament on/near the Strip for the upcoming period.
  • Confirmed re-entry rules for WPT events Dec 10–21 (structure-sheet chunks not fully read).
  • Fixed hotel room rates for specific dates (rates are dynamic).
  • Confirmed fees for Resorts World, Mardi Gras, Renaissance, Circus Circus.
  • A verified 8 a.m. tournament start.
