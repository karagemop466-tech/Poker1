## Recommended hotels (best balance of cost, quality, distance)

1. **Wynn Las Vegas** — best distance (on-site, 0 min walk to the poker room), highest quality (Forbes 4-star), free self-parking for guests. Trade-off: premium dynamic room rate; $55+tax resort fee (~$62.36 fixed/night). Best if cost is secondary to convenience and quality.
2. **Encore at Wynn** — connected to Wynn (0 min), larger standard rooms (745 sq ft vs 640), same $55+tax fee and free self-parking. Slightly more modern rooms.
3. **The Venetian Resort** — across the street (~5–8 min walk via pedestrian bridge), all-suite, high quality. Trade-off: $20/day self-parking for hotel guests (fixed ~$82.36/night extras). Best if you want suite space and are willing to pay $20 parking.

## Notes
- Room rates are dynamic; a Wynn snapshot showed a low of ~$176 (Dec 17/22/23, 2026) and peaks well above $1,000 around events/New Year. Always check the official booking page for your exact dates and confirm whether the displayed rate already includes the resort fee and whether tax is extra.
- Wynn and Encore self-parking is FREE for registered guests (up to 2 vehicles) — this materially lowers true cost versus Venetian/Palazzo ($20/day).
- Tax (13.38% Clark County lodging tax) applies to both room rate and resort fee.
- "True cost per night" in this table excludes the variable room rate; add your quoted room rate + 13.38% tax to get the full nightly total.
