4Executive Summary

Las Vegas Strip poker tournament & hotel planner for a planned vacation. Research date: 2026-11-16. All values verified against official venue sources where available; everything else is flagged.

Bottom line up front

No single-entry tournament is confirmed from an official Las Vegas Strip source for the upcoming period. Every event verifiable on the official Wynn Las Vegas site and its official WPT schedule/structure sheets — including the WPT World Championship (Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026) and all Wynn daily tournaments — permits re-entry, rebuy, and/or add-on. These are listed in a separate section on the Tournaments page.

1) Tournament findings

  • Anchor tournament: WPT World Championship at Wynn Las Vegas, Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026. Official schedule and structure sheets confirm every event allows re-entry (unlimited re-entry until level 9/10, or one entry + one re-entry per starting flight for WPT Prime). None qualify as single entry.
  • Wynn daily tournaments: $200 NLH (Mon–Thu 12pm), $240 NLH Rebuy & Add-on (Fri 12pm), $200 NLH (Sat–Sun 12pm), $160 NLH nightly (6pm). All allow re-entry and/or add-on.
  • Single-entry status: Not confirmed from any official Strip source. The only lead is Westgate's $70 Daily NLH "Freezeout" on a third-party calendar — unverified and off-strip.
  • Cost-to-enter vs prize pool: Highest-guarantee verified events are the WPT World Championship Day 1 flights ($10,400, $1,000,000 added), WPT Prime Championship ($1,100, $5,000,000), and Super Gold Million Mystery Bounty ($400, $3,000,000) — but all are re-entry events.

2) Hotel findings

  • Best hotel (distance + quality): Wynn Las Vegas — on-site (0-min walk to poker room), Forbes 4-star, free guest self-parking, $55+tax (~$62.36) nightly resort fee. Room rate is dynamic.
  • Best connected alternative: Encore at Wynn (connected, 0-min walk; larger rooms; same fee and free parking).
  • Best value alternative: The Venetian Resort — across the street (~5–8 min walk), all-suite, but $20/day guest self-parking brings fixed extras to ~$82.36/night.
  • True cost per night: room rate (dynamic, quote on official booking page) + resort fee + 13.38% Clark County tax + parking. Wynn/Encore fixed extras ~$62.36/night; Venetian/Palazzo ~$82.36/night.

3) Play-all-day schedule

  • No verified 8 a.m. start exists. Earliest verified tournament starts at 10–11 a.m. (WPT flights) or 12 p.m. (Wynn daily).
  • Multiple Day 1 flights overlap, so you can realistically play one main tournament plus possibly a late-night turbo per day.
  • Because all verified events allow re-entry, no single-entry all-day schedule can be built yet.

4) Decision prerequisites (what to confirm before booking)

  • Confirm single-entry/freezeout status with each poker room directly (see Questions & Answers).
  • Quote hotel room rates on official booking pages for exact dates; confirm whether the displayed rate includes resort fee and tax.
  • Review every item on the Flagged for Review page before making decisions.

Verification standard

Every table row carries a source link to an official venue site where available. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank or marked UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
