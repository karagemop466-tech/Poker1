Executive Summary

Bottom line up front

There are no single-entry tournaments confirmed from an official Las Vegas Strip source for the upcoming period. Every event I could verify on the official Wynn Las Vegas site and its official WPT schedule/structure sheets — including the WPT World Championship (Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026) and all Wynn daily tournaments — permits re-entry, rebuy, and/or add-on. These are listed in the separate "Re-Entry / Add-On" section on the Tournaments page.

Key findings

  • Anchor tournament: WPT World Championship at Wynn Las Vegas, Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026. Official schedule and structure sheets confirm every event allows re-entry (unlimited re-entry until level 9/10, or one re-entry per starting flight for WPT Prime). None qualify as single entry.
  • Wynn daily tournaments: $200 NLH (Mon–Thu 12pm), $240 NLH Rebuy & Add-on (Fri 12pm), $200 NLH (Sat–Sun 12pm), $160 NLH nightly (6pm). All allow re-entry and/or add-on.
  • Single-entry status: Not confirmed from any official Strip source. The only lead is Westgate's $70 Daily NLH "Freezeout" on a third-party calendar — unverified and off-strip. Flagged for review.
  • Best hotel (distance + quality): Wynn Las Vegas — on-site (0-min walk to the poker room), Forbes 4-star, free guest self-parking, $55+tax (~$62.36) nightly resort fee. Room rates are dynamic.
  • Best hotel (value alternative): The Venetian Resort — across the street (~5–8 min walk), all-suite, but $20/day guest self-parking brings fixed extras to ~$82.36/night.
  • True cost per night: room rate (dynamic, must be quoted on the official booking page) + resort fee + 13.38% Clark County tax + parking. Wynn/Encore fixed extras ~$62.36/night; Venetian/Palazzo ~$82.36/night (includes $20 parking).
  • Play-all-day: Wynn's first tournament starts at 12 p.m. (or 10–11 a.m. during WPT series flights). No verified 8 a.m. start exists on the current Strip schedule; earliest practical start is ~10–11 a.m.

What you should do before booking

  • Confirm single-entry status with each poker room directly (see Questions & Answers).
  • Quote hotel room rates on the official booking pages for your exact dates; confirm whether the displayed rate includes the resort fee and tax.
  • Review the Flagged for Review page for every unverified value before making decisions.

Verification standard
