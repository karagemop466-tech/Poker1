2Hotels Within Walking Distance

Anchor venue: Wynn Las Vegas poker room, 3131 S Las Vegas Blvd (North Strip) — verified tournament venue (WPT World Championship Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026). Walking standard: reasonable = within ~30 minutes (~1.5 miles). Priority: cost and quality over distance, but distance must be reasonable.

How "true cost per night" is built

True cost per night = room rate + nightly resort fee + tax on the fee + nightly parking. Room rates are dynamic (change by date, room type, view, and demand) and were not fixed from an official booking engine, so the room-rate column is blank where not officially confirmed; the "fixed extras" column shows resort fee + tax + parking only. Add your quoted room rate + 13.38% Clark County lodging tax for the full nightly total.

Recommended hotels

Best overall

Wynn Las Vegas

Best distance (on-site, 0-min walk to the poker room) and highest quality (Forbes 4-star). Free guest self-parking lowers true cost. Trade-off: premium dynamic room rate; $55+tax (~$62.36) nightly resort fee. Best if convenience and quality outweigh room cost.

Best connected alternative

Encore at Wynn

Connected to Wynn (0-min walk). Larger standard rooms (745 sq ft vs 640 at Wynn). Same $55+tax fee and free guest self-parking.

Value alternative

The Venetian Resort

Across the street (~5–8 min walk via pedestrian bridge), all-suite, high quality. Trade-off: $20/day guest self-parking brings fixed extras to ~$82.36/night — about $20 more than Wynn with a car.

Notes

  • Room rates are dynamic; a Wynn snapshot showed a low of ~$176 (Dec 17/22/23, 2026) and peaks well above $1,000 around events/New Year. Always quote on the official booking page for your exact dates and confirm whether the displayed rate includes the resort fee and whether tax is extra.
  • Wynn and Encore self-parking is free for registered guests (up to 2 vehicles) — this materially lowers true cost versus Venetian/Palazzo ($20/day).
  • Clark County lodging tax is 13.38% and applies to both the room rate and the resort fee.
