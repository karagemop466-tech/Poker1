REQUIRED ANSWERS BEFORE A REASONABLE CHOICE

Open questions checklist

01

What are the trip dates and length?

Specific nights determine tournament availability, nightly room price, and what can be compared.

02

What does “single entry” mean to you?

One paid entry total, no re-entry after busting, no extra starting flights, or one entry per event? These are materially different rules.

03

What is the buy-in ceiling and total tournament budget?

Guarantee-to-buy-in is not expected return. Establish a maximum event buy-in and total exposure independently.

04

Who is eligible for age- or format-restricted events?

Some scheduled events explicitly require age 50+; tag-team, satellite, bounty, and other formats can change suitability.

05

What is the hotel budget / room standard / cancellation requirement?

Without occupancy, room type, and cancellation preferences, “best quality and cost” cannot be ranked honestly.

06

Is the all-day objective literal, and are late finishes acceptable?

No verified 8 AM event is in this shortlist, and tournament end times cannot be guaranteed from the posted schedule.

PROVISIONAL PATH

What can be recommended today

  1. Use the Venetian schedule as the most actionable near-term lead, then ask the venue to confirm one-entry/no-re-entry rules for the specific one-day event numbers.
  2. If the entry rule is acceptable, compare candidates by buy-in, published guarantee, and format; the simple guarantee/buy-in multiple is only a screening statistic.
  3. For hotel convenience, request same-date Venetian / Palazzo and Wynn quotes with the $55 + tax fee included in the total; the Venetian is on-property to its own room.
  4. Do not build an hourly multi-event itinerary until the venues confirm registration cutoffs, session durations, and an actual schedule of eligible events.
