05 / FLAGGED FOR REVIEW

Questions to resolve before deciding

Unknowns are intentionally visible—not silently guessed.

Trip details

  • What are the arrival and departure dates? Necessary for live schedules, availability, and nightly totals.
  • How many guests / rooms? Changes occupancy pricing and hotel selection.
  • What is the lodging and poker budget? Needed to define value and bankroll exposure.
  • Are there game, age, or accessibility preferences? Needed to filter events and properties.

Event evidence

  • Does the official schedule show an event in the future and registration status?
  • Is the buy-in the full mandatory entry cost, including fees?
  • Are re-entry, re-buy, add-on, and re-entry windows expressly ruled out?
  • Is the guarantee official, and what happens if the field misses it?
  • Are start time, late registration, duration, and breaks confirmed?

Hotel & logistics

  • Official quote for exact stay dates, occupancy, taxes, resort fee, and cancellation terms?
  • Does the resort fee incur tax, and are there mandatory destination charges?
  • Walking route/time to each selected poker room, including venue entrance location?
  • Can consecutive events be reached with adequate transition time?

Decision approach

  • Compare confirmed guaranteed prize pool per dollar only when comparable format/terms are disclosed; it is not expected value.
  • Prefer a single initial entry with no re-entry/rebuy/add-on; put spend-to-advantage formats in a separate list.
  • Score hotels first on all-in cost and quality, then require a walk of 30 minutes or less.
  • Re-check the official schedule and hotel checkout total immediately before booking.
