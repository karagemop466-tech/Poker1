01

Most useful near-term tournament lead

The Venetian’s official DeepStack Extravaganza IV runs Nov 16–Dec 23, 2026 and advertises more than $3.6M in guaranteed tournament prize pools. Wynn’s WPT World Championship is scheduled for Nov 27–Dec 21. Both schedules include multi-flight events; the reviewed entries do not establish a single-entry rule. Treat Venetian one-day events as candidates, not confirmed matches.

02

Best practical hotel lead

The Venetian/Palazzo poker room is on property at the Palazzo; that removes an outdoor walk to this series, but no precise internal walking time is published. Official poker-room rates are advertised from $119 at Venetian / $209 at Palazzo, with availability, blackout dates, qualifying play, and a $55 nightly resort fee plus tax. These are not a stay-specific total.

03

Schedule reality check

No reviewed event begins at 8:00 AM. The Venetian schedule’s reviewed starts are 10:10 or 11:10 AM, and multi-hour tournament duration and finish times are not guaranteed. Multiple same-day entries cannot be presumed to fit sequentially. An all-day 8 AM-to-late schedule is therefore not currently supportable.
